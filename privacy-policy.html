Privacy Policy for ISHTO

Effective Date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ISHTO ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you play the ISHTO mobile game on Android.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication & Profile Data: If you sign in via Google Play Games or Google Sign-In, we collect your public gamer tag/display name and a unique user ID to maintain your player profile, cloud saves, and multiplayer matchmaking.
  • Gameplay & Match Data: In-game coin balance, match statistics, unlocked pawns, cowrie themes, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs, performance telemetry, and device identifiers collected by Google Play Services and Google AdMob to prevent fraud and maintain app stability.
  • In-App Purchases: Order tokens and purchase history handled securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide real-time online multiplayer matchmaking and private friend rooms.
  • To save and synchronize your game progress, theme unlocks, and coin balances.
  • To serve rewarded and non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Families Policies.
  • To diagnose technical bugs and improve gameplay performance.

3. Third-Party Services

ISHTO integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Database)

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your gameplay data only for as long as necessary to provide game services. You can delete your account and associated cloud data at any time directly in the app via Settings → Account & Data → Delete Account, or via our web deletion portal at Account & Data Deletion.

5. Children's Privacy

ISHTO complies with Google Play Families Policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental safeguards.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data rights, contact us at: support@ishto.game
